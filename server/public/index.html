QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Where can I check my reservation?

Open Explore events and scroll to My bookings. Your reservation status and any available payment steps are shown there.

What should I do after making a transfer?

Open the relevant booking and submit your payment confirmation. The event organizer will review it and update your booking status.

How do I follow up on a support request?

Submit a request using the form below. Select your ticket from the list to read the conversation and send a reply.

My account is waiting for approval. What now?

An administrator must review new account requests before sign-in is enabled. Return to StuHub and try again after you receive confirmation that your account was approved.
